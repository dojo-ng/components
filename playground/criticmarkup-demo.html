rich-text-criticmarkup — demo

CriticMarkup tracked changes for <dj-rich-text>: {++inserted++}, {--deleted--}, {~~old~>new~~} (a substitution), {>>comment<<} (bare or anchored), and {==highlighted==}. The seeded paragraph below has one of each, an anchored comment, and a same-kind nested pair (detected and refused as its own node, never mangled — try finding it in the raw value). Headings, lists, and Markdown are loaded alongside it, so marks compose with the rest of the document.

Toolbar: Suggest edits toggles suggestion mode (typed/deleted text becomes a mark instead of a direct edit — try it, then split or merge a paragraph with Enter/Backspace to see the structural policy below in action); Accept/Reject act on the mark under the caret; Accept all/Reject all resolve the whole document (behind a confirm popup); Add comment inserts a bare comment at a collapsed caret, or anchors one over a selection. Click the in-content comment button (or a highlighted comment) to edit or delete a note — "Remove note" keeps the highlight, "Remove highlight" drops both.
