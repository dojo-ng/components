Data grid columns: 30 columns, 10,000 rows

Drag a header's end edge to resize it (double-click the edge to fit), drag a header to move it, or open the ⋮ menu on a header for Wider, Narrower, Move, Pin, Hide, and the column chooser. The layout is saved in this browser and restored when you reload.

  • Keyboard: Tab to the grid, ArrowUp to the header row, Left and Right to pick a column. Space grabs it, Left and Right move it, Space drops it. Alt+ArrowDown opens its menu.
  • In the body, Left and Right scroll the columns sideways. Pinned columns stay put.
