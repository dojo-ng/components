The tree is the menu, the cards and the top nav are links, and the view on the right is chosen only from the published location. Skim a few pages quickly, wait for the dwell to pass on the last one, then press Back: the skimmed pages are skipped. Cmd/Ctrl-click a card, a nav link or a tree row to open it in a new tab.

Hash mode: the route lives after the #, so this page works under python3 -m http.server with no SPA fallback, and a reload on #/orders/7 lands on that view. Edit the hash by hand and press Enter: the view follows. Try: Forward lights up after Back and goes dark after a new click; click a middle breadcrumb and the tree reselects that node; scroll down the long page, navigate, then press Back to land at the same place; after a navigation the view title takes focus; type in the notes box, then press Back or a link to be asked before leaving, and close the tab to see the browser's own prompt.
